Auto-mpg dataset
Kaggle link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/uciml/autompg-dataset
Modes: OLS models
two_line_org:  Two line solutions regressions equations models with regressor variable origin considered as a categorical variable.
two_line_org_bc: Two line solutions regressions equations models apply box-cox transformations on y & regressor variable origin considered as a categorical    
                 variable. 
one_line_org: Single line solutions regressions equations models only origin considered as a categorical variable. 
one_line_org_bc:  Single line solutions regressions equations models apply box-cox transformations on y & only origin considered as a categorical variable. 
one_line_org_cyl: Single line solutions regressions equations models now regressor variable origin & cylinder considered as a categorical
two_line_org_cyl: Two line solutions regressions equations models now regressor variable origin & cylinder considered as a categorical
one_line_org_cyl_bc: Two line solutions regressions equations models apply box-cox transformations on y & regressor variable origin & cylinder considered as a categorical
