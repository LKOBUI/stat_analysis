- mpg (miles per gallon)
- displacement (engine size)
- horsepower (engine power)
- weight (vehicle weight)
- acceleration (0–60 mph time)

These are categories or counts:
- cylinders (number of cylinders: 3, 4, 5, 6, 8)
- model year (treated as discrete, though numeric)
- origin (1 - USA, 2- Europe, 3-Japan)

- Residuals vs. Fitted Values Plot
Checks whether residuals are randomly scattered. Patterns suggest non‑linearity or heteroscedasticity.
- Standardized Residuals vs. Fitted Values Plot
Same as above but scaled residuals, making it easier to spot outliers.
- Studentized Residuals Plot
Highlights extreme residuals more clearly, useful for identifying influential points.
- Histogram of Residuals
Shows whether residuals are approximately normally distributed.
- Q–Q Plot (Quantile–Quantile Plot)
Compares residual distribution against a normal distribution. Deviations from the diagonal line indicate non‑normality.
- Residuals vs. Predictor Variables Plot
Helps detect non‑linear relationships with individual predictors.
- Scale–Location Plot (Spread vs. Fitted)
Plots square root of standardized residuals vs. fitted values. Useful for checking homoscedasticity.
- Lag Plot of Residuals (for time series)
