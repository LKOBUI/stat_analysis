Transformations and Weighting to Correct Model Inadequacies
 - Sets the stage for why transformations and weighting are essential when model assumptions are violated.
 - Variance-Stabilizing Transformations
   - Techniques to correct heteroscedasticity (non-constant variance) in residuals.
   - Common methods include square root, log, and reciprocal transformations.
 - Transformations to Linearize the Model
 - Analytical Methods for Selecting a Transformation
   - Transformations on Y: The Box–Cox Method
   - Adjusts predictors to linearize relationships or reduce skewness.
